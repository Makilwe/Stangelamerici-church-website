Sacraments

Seven encounters with Christ.

The sacraments are at the heart of our parish life. Through them, Christ Himself meets us with His grace.

✦

Baptism

The first sacrament — we are washed clean of sin and welcomed into God’s family.

✝

Confirmation

Sealed with the Holy Spirit, we are strengthened to live and share our faith.

✚

Eucharist

The Body and Blood of Christ — the source and summit of our parish life.

❤

Reconciliation

Confess your sins and receive God’s mercy and peace. Saturdays 4:00 – 5:00 PM, or by appointment.

✡

Anointing of the Sick

Comfort, healing, and strength for those who are seriously ill or facing surgery.

◈

Holy Orders

The sacrament of ordination to the diaconate and the priesthood.

◆

Matrimony

A covenant of love blessed by God. Engaged couples, please contact the office well in advance.

Arranging a Sacrament

To arrange a Baptism, First Communion, Confirmation, wedding, or Anointing of the Sick, please call the parish office at (718) 293-0984. We are happy to guide you through each step.
