Parish Council

The Parish Council supports the mission of St. Angela Merici Catholic Church by offering guidance, leadership, and collaboration in the spiritual and pastoral life of our community.

Our Mission

The Parish Council works together with parish leadership to:

• Strengthen parish life
• Support ministries and programs
• Encourage participation and service
• Promote unity and communication
• Help discern the needs of the parish community

Working Together

The Parish Council meets regularly to discuss parish needs, upcoming events, ministry support, and ways to help our community grow in faith and unity. Parishioners are welcome to share ideas or concerns with any council member.
