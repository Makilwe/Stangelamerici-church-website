Ministries

Share your time and talent.

Our parish is blessed with many ministries that serve at Mass and care for our community. There is a place for you here.

Eucharistic Ministers

Extraordinary Ministers of Holy Communion assist at Mass and bring Communion to the sick and homebound.

Leader: Claudina Garcia

✦

Altar Servers

Young people and adults who serve at the altar during Mass and special liturgies.

📖

Lectors

Proclaim the Word of God at Mass and parish celebrations.

𝅘𝅥𝅮

Music Ministry

Choirs and musicians lift our hearts in song at Sunday Masses in English and Spanish.

Youth Ministry

Faith, friendship, and fun for our young people — every Friday at 6:00 PM in the lower church.

Ushers

Welcome parishioners, help with seating, and care for the needs of the assembly.

Food Pantry

Serving our neighbors in need. Contact the parish office for distribution days and ways to help.

Equipo Bautismal

Preparation and celebration of baptisms in our parish.
