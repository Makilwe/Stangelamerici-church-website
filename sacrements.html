The Sacraments

The Sacraments are powerful signs of God’s grace, instituted by Christ and entrusted to the Church. Through them, we receive divine life and are strengthened in our journey of faith.

Baptism

Baptism is the first Sacrament of initiation and the gateway to the Christian life. To schedule a Baptism, please contact the parish office.

Confirmation

Confirmation strengthens us with the gifts of the Holy Spirit and completes our initiation into the Church. Preparation is offered through our Religious Education program.

Holy Eucharist

The Eucharist is the source and summit of our faith. Join us for Mass and receive the Body and Blood of Christ.

Reconciliation (Confession)

Through Confession, we receive God’s mercy and forgiveness. Confessions are available every Saturday from 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM or by appointment.

Anointing of the Sick

This Sacrament brings healing, strength, and peace to those who are ill or preparing for surgery. Please call the parish office for a visit.

Holy Matrimony

Marriage is a sacred covenant between husband and wife. Couples wishing to marry should contact the parish at least six months in advance.

Holy Orders

Holy Orders is the Sacrament through which men are ordained as deacons, priests, or bishops. If you feel called to serve, speak with a priest or visit the Archdiocese vocation office.
