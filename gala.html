17th Annual Dinner Gala

September 27, 2026

“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labor in vain.” — Psalm 127:1

Each year our parish family gathers for an evening of dinner, fellowship, and gratitude — celebrating the people and the faith that make St. Angela Merici home.

2026 Honoree

Mr. Kenneth C. Roe

Class of 1955

Mr. Kenneth C. Roe, St. Angela’s Class of 1955, was honored at the 2026 Annual Dinner. He and his late wife Marilyn received five sacraments at St. Angela’s, from Baptism through Matrimony. An altar boy from 1952 to 1963, a CYO athlete, a CCD teacher, and a coach, he calls his years growing up at St. Angela’s his “Golden Days” — and hopes today’s parishioners will one day look back and feel the same.

In many ways, the church was the center of our social lives.
